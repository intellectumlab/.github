## Intellectum Lab

Production AI and data systems for regulated finance in the EU — **MiCA, DORA, the EU AI Act** — turned into systems
that pass validation: order-book and trade records in ESMA format, Travel Rule, DORA registers and resilience,
governance over AI in AML and monitoring.

We publish what can be checked, so you don't have to take our word for it.

| Repository | What it is |
|---|---|
| [**midas-precheck**](https://github.com/intellectumlab/midas-precheck) | Check MiCA MIDAS files (`auth.117` record keeping, `auth.118` order book) against ESMA's published naming rule, JSON schemas and MET/QUA rules — on your own machine. [In-browser version](https://intellectumlab.com/midas-precheck.html). |
| [**steploop**](https://github.com/intellectumlab/steploop) | A durable agent loop in dependency-free Python, plus a public reference run against three pinned EU texts (Travel Rule, DORA ITS, MiCAR record-keeping RTS) — published with its gaps. |

Every repository says what it does **not** prove. If you find a place where we misread a regulation, open an issue:
that is the most useful report we can get.
